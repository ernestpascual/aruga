## Slide 2: The Everyday Struggle
- **Title**: The Home Program Black Hole
- **Subtitle**: What happens between clinic sessions is where recovery stalls.
- **3 Key Realities**:
  1. **Lost Handouts**: 70% of clients lose or ignore paper exercise sheets. Patients get confused without active guidance.
  2. **Unpaid Admin Time**: Therapists spend 15–20 minutes per patient after hours on calls, emails, and manual notes.
  3. **Unseen Compensations**: Patients practice with poor form—hiking shoulders or leaning trunks—and cement bad habits.
- **Key Stat**: **73%** of clinicians cite documentation burden and chasing home compliance as their #1 driver of burnout.

## Slide 3: 3 Simple Steps. Complete Control.
- **Title**: How Aruga Works
- **Subtitle**: A digital co-pilot that keeps the licensed clinician in the driver's seat.
- **Workflow**:
  1. **You Prescribe (30s)**: Type rough shorthand. Aruga formats it into a clear 3-step home program with patient-ready instructions.
  2. **Patient Records (30s)**: Clients record a quick video of their tabletop drill. If fatigued, they log a quick reason why.
  3. **You Sign Off (3m)**: Smart tracking spots key movements and pre-drafts your SOAP notes. You review, edit, and publish.
- **Golden Rule**: **Human-in-the-Loop**. Aruga never sends feedback to patients automatically. AI only creates private drafts for your clinical approval.

## Slide 4: Objective Tracking (No Endless Video)
- **Title**: Objective Tracking (No Endless Video)

## Slide 5: Multi-Box SOAP Notes
- **Title**: Multi-Box SOAP Notes
- **Subtitle**: Structured for live sessions and home program reviews.
- **4 Clear Domains**:
  - **S (Subjective)**: Fatigue level, pain score (0–10), and client comfort feedback.
  - **O (Objective)**: Reps completed, hold duration, grasp aperture, and movement range.
  - **A (Assessment)**: Motor recovery progress and compensatory trunk habits.
  - **P (Plan)**: Weight progression, rest intervals, and next appointment focus.
- **Clinical Freedom**: Works across in-clinic live sessions and home program reviews. Saves **~70%** of documentation time.
