Aruga — Brand & Messaging Guide

Care that continuesHome practice, measuredClinician-directed

Aruga (Filipino: to take care of, to nurture) — care that continues beyond the clinic walls. The enemy is the invisible home program: unobserved practice that silently fails 50–65% of the time. The hero is the licensed therapist; the AI is the instrument.

3.1 · AGENTIC

Agents that prepare evidence, not opinions that replace clinicians

Bounded agents (Activity · Biomech Planner · Goal · Highlights) execute multi-step clinical-prep work with inspectable traces; therapists publish or discard.

Say: Agents prepare, therapists publish · inspectable traces

Never: autonomous treatment · AI therapist · the agent decides

3.2 · SIMULATION

Replay the movement. Rehearse the recovery.

Every capture becomes a replayable movement simulation: SAM overlay + 3D dot-body synced to the scrub bar; U1–U11 views re-filtered from one capture — no re-record, no re-bill.

Say: movement replay · one capture, every view

Never: virtual reality therapy · digital patient simulation

3.3 · DIGITAL TWINS

A movement twin that grows with every practice

Use verbatim: a movement digital twin — the evolving record of how their body moves (3D keypoints, angle trajectories, ROM trends, flags). Kinematic, not diagnostic.

Say: movement digital twin · your twin remembers

Never: digital twin of the patient (unqualified)

3.4 · HYPERPERSONALIZATION

No two patients get the same judgment

Goals re-validated after every session; insights & Highlights synthesized from the individual's own twin, in their family's language. Personalization of judgment, not just content.

Say: re-judged after every session · written from your own data

Never: AI knows what's best for you

3.5 · ASYNC TELEREHAB

Care that doesn't need both of you awake

The full loop (prescribe → practice → measure → validate → feedback → story) is store-and-forward. No scheduling, no wearables, any phone.

Say: asynchronous by design · supervision without scheduling

Never: offline · delayed care (frame as virtue)

Paste-Ready Copy

10 seconds: "Therapists can't see home practice, so 50–65% of it silently fails. Aruga turns the patient's own phone video into a measured movement twin — goals re-judged after every session, progress families can read. The therapist always decides."

30 seconds: "In 2016 we designed SPOTx because Philippine therapists were losing patients between sessions. The problem was never effort — home practice is invisible, and invisible practice fails. Aruga makes it visible: SAM 3.1 and a 3D body model measure every recorded session, agents re-validate every goal, and each family's progress story is written from their own movement. Asynchronous, phone-first, clinician-directed. Care that continues."

Objections: "AI medical advice?" → measures and drafts; therapists decide and publish (architectural). "Accuracy?" → trend-grade ±4–6° literature bands, bad captures suppressed, therapist verifies. "Why not WhatsApp?" → where records and privacy go to die (SPOTx, 2016).
